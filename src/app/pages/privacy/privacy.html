Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

InterviewPrep AI respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information used to provide authentication and interview preparation services.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an InterviewPrep AI account, we may collect information such as:

  • Your full name
  • Your email address
  • Your experience information
  • Your account role

Passwords are not stored in plain text.

2. Sign in with Google

InterviewPrep AI provides Sign in with Google as an optional authentication method.

When you use Sign in with Google, InterviewPrep AI may receive:

  • Your Google account email address
  • Your name
  • A unique Google account identifier used to associate your Google account with your InterviewPrep AI account

InterviewPrep AI does not receive or store your Google account password.

InterviewPrep AI does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, contacts, or other private Google account content.
